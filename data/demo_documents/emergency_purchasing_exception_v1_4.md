# Permitted use

An emergency exception may be used only when immediate action is necessary to protect health, safety, legal compliance, physical assets, cybersecurity, or continuity of a critical service and the normal procurement timeline would materially increase the harm.

# Minimum approvals

The accountable executive and Procurement must approve the emergency path. Information Security, Privacy, Legal, or Finance approval remains required when the underlying risk trigger applies, although the review may be time-bounded and completed in parallel when necessary.

# Retrospective record

Within five business days, the requester must complete the intake record, document why normal sourcing was impractical, attach the authorization, record the final spend, and route unresolved conditions for follow-up.
