# Intake checklist

The requester must provide the business need, accountable sponsor, estimated annual spend, funding source, requested start date, service description, data-access profile, system-access profile, and known alternatives.

# Competitive sourcing

Three competitive quotes are normally required when estimated annual spend is $50,000 or more. Procurement may document a sole-source rationale when competition is not practical.

# Due diligence

Procurement verifies tax and registration details, sanctions and exclusion lists, conflicts of interest, financial viability, insurance requirements, and duplicate supplier records. Material concerns are assigned to the appropriate specialist for review.

# Approval record

The intake record must show each required approval, the approving role, the approval date, unresolved conditions, and the final purchasing disposition. Email alone is not the system of record.
