# Review trigger

A third-party security review is required before a vendor receives company credentials, connects to the company network, hosts or processes company data, or provides software that supports a material business process.

# Evidence requirements

The review may require a current independent assurance report, penetration-test summary, vulnerability-management evidence, incident-response commitments, encryption details, identity and access controls, subcontractor disclosures, and a remediation plan for material findings.

# Decision

Information Security records an approve, approve-with-conditions, or reject recommendation. Approval with conditions must identify an owner and due date for each condition. Procurement may not treat an unanswered questionnaire as approval.
