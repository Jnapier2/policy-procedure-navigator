# Trigger

Privacy review is required when a vendor will collect, access, process, store, disclose, or transmit personal data for the company.

# Required information

The requester must identify the data categories, data subjects, business purpose, processing locations, retention period, subprocessors, deletion method, and cross-border transfer mechanism.

# Contract control

A data processing addendum is required when the vendor acts as a processor or service provider. Privacy conditions must be resolved or assigned before the contract is approved.
