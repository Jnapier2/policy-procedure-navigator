# Purpose

This policy establishes the approvals and controls required before the company engages a new vendor or renews a material vendor relationship.

# Required approvals before engagement

A business owner must document the business need, identify an accountable business sponsor, and submit the procurement intake before making a commitment to a prospective vendor.

Procurement approval is required for every new vendor. Procurement coordinates vendor due diligence, sanctions screening, financial-risk review, and confirmation that the vendor record does not duplicate an existing supplier.

Finance approval is required when expected annual spend is $25,000 or more, when spend is not included in the approved budget, or when the payment structure creates a material prepayment or concentration risk.

Legal review is required for non-standard contract terms, intellectual-property transfers, material indemnity obligations, regulated services, or agreements that cannot use the approved contract template.

Privacy review is required when the vendor will collect, access, process, store, or transmit personal data on the company's behalf.

Information Security review is required when the vendor will access company systems, receive company credentials, host company data, connect to the company network, or provide software used for a material business process.

# Contracting and purchasing gate

Only an authorized signatory may bind the company. The vendor must not begin work, receive production credentials, or invoice the company until the agreement is signed and the required purchase order is issued.

# Exceptions

Emergency exceptions must follow the Emergency Purchasing Exception Procedure. An emergency does not eliminate retrospective review, documentation, or conflict-of-interest requirements.
